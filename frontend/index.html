About BLW

Banaras Locomotive Works (BLW), formerly known as Diesel Locomotive Works (DLW), is one of the premier locomotive manufacturing units of Indian Railways located in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.

Important Milestones

  • Foundation Stone : 23 April 1956
  • Established : August 1961
  • First Broad Gauge Locomotive : January 1964
  • Export of Locomotives to Various Countries
  • Production of Modern Electric Locomotives

Technical Training Centre (TTC)

TTC provides training for BLW employees, railway officers, apprentices and engineering students.
